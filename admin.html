What does BAMSEC mean?

BAMSEC stands for Basic Medical Sciences Student Electoral Commission. It is the commission name used on this portal for the administration and conduct of the Basic Medical Sciences Faculty Election.

The commission’s work should be guided by fairness, transparency, accountability, confidentiality, equal treatment of eligible students and adherence to the approved election rules. Administrative access must be used only for authorised election duties.

Election workflow: do these in order

  1. Set up the ballot. Add all approved positions and confirm their display order.
  2. Register candidates. Verify each candidate’s name, department, matric number, photograph and manifesto.
  3. Upload the voter register. Import the approved voter list and check duplicates, spelling, matric numbers and contact information.
  4. Review PIN readiness. Confirm eligible voters and contact details. Replacing a PIN invalidates the previous PIN.
  5. Authorise opening. Open only after the ballot and voter register have been reviewed and authorised officials agree it is ready.
  6. Close and publish. Close voting at the approved time. Display results only after verification and authorisation.
  7. Archive and report. Preserve the election record. Save an archive before using any reset function.

Important safeguards

  • Never share administrator passwords or a voter’s PIN.
  • Issue or replace PINs only when necessary; replacing one may immediately invalidate the old PIN.
  • Do not change the voter register or candidate list after voting starts unless the authorised process permits it.
  • Do not publish results before the electoral chairman authorises publication.
  • Reset Everything is destructive. Archive first and use it only when explicitly authorised.
  • This portal supports administration; it does not replace the approved constitution, election guidelines or decisions of the authorised electoral body.
